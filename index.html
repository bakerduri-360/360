What we do

Three disciplines that used to need three contractors. Now they don't.

Most properties end up with a patchwork: one company runs the cameras, another handles the router and WiFi dead zones, a third bolts on smart locks that never quite talk to anything else. When something breaks, no one takes the call.

360dgree exists to close that gap. We treat surveillance, networking, and home automation as one system — because in practice, that's exactly what they are. Your cameras need bandwidth. Your smart locks need a stable network. Your automations need both to work, every time.

One assessment. One design. One team on-site. One number to call after.
